## 2. Rescheduling Policy
- **Free Rescheduling:** Customers can reschedule appointments free of charge up to **1 hour before the scheduled start time** directly through the App via **Booking History > Reschedule Booking**.
- **Max Reschedule Limit:** A booking may be rescheduled up to 3 times within a 7-day window. Beyond this, the booking may be automatically cancelled.

---

## 3. Refund Processing Timelines & Modes
1. **Source Account Refunds:** All approved refunds are automatically initiated back to the original payment source (UPI VPA, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking).
2. **Standard Turnaround Times:**
   - **UPI / Instant Wallets:** 1 to 2 business hours (max 24 hours).
   - **Net Banking & Debit Cards:** 3 to 5 banking days.
   - **Credit Cards:** 5 to 7 billing cycle business days depending on the issuing bank.
3. **Cash on Delivery (COD) Bookings:** If a refund is approved for a COD order (e.g. warranty re-inspection claim), the refund is processed via direct UPI transfer to the verified mobile number or Bank Account IMPS.

---

## 4. Disputed Jobs & Unsatisfactory Service Claims
1. **30-Day Guarantee Claims:** If you are dissatisfied with a repair or the appliance exhibits the same fault within 30 days, we provide a free re-visit and rework by an expert senior technician.
2. **Escalation to Refund Review Committee:** If rework fails to resolve the issue, you may request an investigation. If deemed justifiable by the Safety & Quality Review board, a full or partial refund of service labor charges will be authorized within 48 hours.
