# TECHNICIAN PARTNER SERVICE AGREEMENT & CODE OF CONDUCT

**Last Updated:** August 15, 2026  
**Entity:** BookUrTechnician Technologies Private Limited ("BookUrTechnician")  
**Target:** Independent Service Professionals, Tradespeople & Technicians ("Partner", "Technician", "Pro", "you")

---

## 1. Independent Contractor Relationship
1. By registering as a Service Partner on the **BookUrTechnician Pro** application, you enter into an independent contractor service provider relationship with BookUrTechnician.
2. Nothing in this agreement shall create an employer-employee, partnership, or principal-agent relationship.
3. You maintain full flexibility to accept or decline job dispatches based on your real-time availability.

---

## 2. Mandatory Verification & KYC Compliance
1. Prior to activation on the dispatch network, every partner must undergo comprehensive background verification, including:
   - Valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN Card, Driving License).
   - Clean criminal history check and police verification certification.
   - Skill trade certification / technical competency assessment.
2. BookUrTechnician reserves the right to periodically re-audit KYC records and revoke partner status for any falsified documentation.

---

## 3. Payouts, Commissions & Billing Integrity
1. **Commission Model:** BookUrTechnician charges a transparent platform matchmaking fee on completed jobs as specified in your Partner Dashboard.
2. **Weekly Payouts:** Partner earnings (including online customer tips and travel allowances) are settled directly to the registered bank account via automated NEFT/IMPS every Tuesday.
3. **Strict Prohibition of Off-App Overcharging:** Partners must never demand arbitrary cash charges above the standardized in-app rate card for labor or spare parts. Doing so results in immediate blacklisting and forfeiture of pending weekly incentives.

---

## 4. Professional Code of Conduct & Safety
1. **Safety Standards:** Technicians must strictly adhere to safety guidelines (insulated tools, PPE, testing equipment, circuit breakers).
2. **Customer Interaction:** Treat every customer with professionalism, punctuality, and courtesy. Any harassment, intoxication, offensive language, or theft will lead to immediate police reporting and permanent termination.
3. **Privacy Compliance:** Customer phone numbers, addresses, and family details provided in the app are confidential and strictly for job execution. Recording, saving, or contacting customers off-platform after job completion is a severe breach of the DPDP Act and this agreement.
