## 1. Introduction & Scope
BookUrTechnician operates an on-demand hyperlocal home services marketplace connecting consumers ("Customers", "Users", "you") with independent vetted service professionals ("Technician Partners", "Pros"). This Privacy Policy explains in transparent detail how we collect, store, utilize, process, share, transfer, and protect your personal information when you access or use our mobile applications (Customer App, Technician Pro App), website, APIs, or related customer services (collectively, the "Platform").

By accessing, registering, browsing, or utilizing the BookUrTechnician Platform, you explicitly consent to the collection and handling of your information as described in this policy. If you do not agree with any terms of this policy, please refrain from accessing or using our services.

## 2. Personal Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Identity & Contact Data:** Name, mobile phone number, email address, profile photograph, emergency contact details.
- **Address & Location Data:** Home, office, or custom service location addresses, postal pin codes, landmark details, apartment/building numbers.
- **Service Request & Booking Data:** Selected appliance types, brand and model details, fault descriptions, photos/audio notes uploaded to assist diagnosis, scheduled time slots, and special instructions.
- **Payment Information:** Payment methods used (UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, Wallets), billing addresses, transaction references. *Note: Sensitive card credentials (CVV, Card Numbers) are processed directly by RBI-authorized payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay, Stripe) through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant tokenization; we do not store full raw card numbers or CVV on our servers.*
- **Feedback & Support Communication:** Customer service chat logs, support tickets, technician ratings, written reviews, and call recordings conducted through customer support helplines.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Real-Time Geolocation Data:** Precise and approximate GPS location data from your device when the application is open or running in the foreground/background (where permitted) to:
  - Match you with nearby available technicians.
  - Track technician en-route arrival time and display live on-map telemetry.
  - Calculate travel distance, arrival notifications, and dynamic dispatch routing.
- **Device & Technical Telemetry:** Device manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (Android ID, Advertising ID), IP address, network carrier, crash analytics, and app session metadata.
- **Usage & Interaction Data:** Page views, service clicks, category searches, cart additions, time spent on features, and referral links.

### C. Information from Third Parties & Partners
- **Technician Verification & KYC:** Government ID records (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving License), police verification reports, background check clearances, and professional skill certificates provided during partner onboarding.
- **Telephony & Masked Calling:** Call metadata (timestamps, call duration, masked virtual bridge identifiers) when customers and technicians communicate via our privacy-safe masked phone relay.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We process your personal information strictly for legitimate commercial, operational, and legal purposes, including:

1. **Service Fulfillment & Delivery:** Matching service orders with qualified technicians, facilitating door-step appliance repairs, maintenance, and installations.
2. **Real-Time Tracking & Telemetry:** Providing real-time distance estimations, ETA countdowns, and live GPS map tracking.
3. **Privacy Masked Communication:** Facilitating direct, masked voice and in-app chat communications between customers and technicians without exposing personal phone numbers.
4. **Payment Processing & Invoicing:** Processing service charges, spare parts quotations, platform convenience fees, refunds, and generating GST-compliant tax invoices.
5. **Safety, Trust & Quality Assurance:** Conducting technician background checks, monitoring service ratings, verifying OTP completion codes upon job completion, and investigating safety incidents.
6. **Customer Support:** Resolving disputes, scheduling warranty re-inspections, answering queries, and processing refund requests.
7. **Platform Improvement & Fraud Prevention:** Detecting fraudulent bookings, spam abuse, multiple account abuse, and improving algorithmic dispatch efficiency.
8. **Transactional Notifications:** Sending booking confirmation emails/SMS, OTP authentication codes, technician assignment alerts, invoice copies, and warranty expiry reminders.

## 4. How We Share and Disclose Your Information
We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. We share information only under strict confidentiality and security protocols with:

- **Assigned Technician Partners:** When a booking is confirmed, the assigned technician receives your first name, service address, preferred time slot, contact number (or virtual masked number), and uploaded appliance issue details solely to perform the booked service.
- **Authorized Service Providers & Vendors:**
  - **Payment Gateways (Razorpay / Stripe):** For secure checkout, payment verification, and automated refund processing.
  - **Cloud Infrastructure (AWS / Google Cloud):** For encrypted database storage, compute hosting, and server infrastructure.
  - **Communication & Email Delivery (Brevo / Twilio):** For dispatching transactional OTPs, login verification codes, and service invoices.
  - **Maps & Geolocation Services (Google Maps / Mapbox APIs):** For reverse geocoding, distance matrix calculation, and live tracking renders.
  - **Analytics & Crash Reporting (Firebase Crashlytics):** For tracking application stability and bug fixes.
- **Legal & Regulatory Authorities:** When mandated by law, court order, governmental inquiry, or in good faith to protect the safety, rights, or property of BookUrTechnician, our users, or the public.
- **Corporate Restructuring:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, asset sale, or reorganization, user data may be transferred as a business asset subject to the commitments in this Privacy Policy.

## 5. Telephony Privacy & Masked Calling Protections
To protect user privacy and prevent off-platform harassment or unsolicited contact:
- Direct telephone calls between Customers and Technician Partners may be routed through a secure cloud telephony bridge.
- Both parties may see a secure virtual relay number rather than each other’s personal mobile numbers.
- Masked call sessions automatically expire once the service booking is marked as completed or cancelled.

## 6. Data Retention & Storage
- We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, maintain your active account, comply with statutory tax/financial record-keeping requirements (e.g., GST laws requiring 7-year invoice retention), and resolve disputes.
- All database records and telemetry logs are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 / SSL and encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.

## 7. Your Rights & Choices (DPDP Act Compliance)
Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) and applicable data protection regulations, you have the following rights:
1. **Right to Access & Review:** You may review your personal profile, addresses, and transaction history at any time within the App.
2. **Right to Correction & Update:** You can update inaccurate profile information, phone numbers, or addresses directly in the App or via Support.
3. **Right to Account & Data Deletion:** You may request full account deletion and data erasure by navigating to **Profile > Settings > Delete Account** or emailing `privacy@bookurtechnician.com`. Upon verification, all non-statutory personal data will be permanently purged within 30 days.
4. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You can withdraw location tracking permissions or marketing communication consent at any time via your device settings.
5. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** You have the right to lodge a complaint regarding data processing with our designated Grievance Officer.
