1. Free Cancellation Window

Customers may cancel any booking free of charge up to 1 hour before the scheduled appointment slot, or at any time during the active searching state before a technician has accepted the dispatch request.

Cancellation Stage Eligible Refund Amount Cancellation Charge
During "Searching Technician" State 100% of Total Amount Paid ₹0 (Zero Charge)
> 1 Hour Before Scheduled Slot 100% of Base Service Fee Visiting inspection fee retained if tech en-route
After Technician Arrival at Doorstep Base Service Fee refunded Visiting inspection fee retained (₹49)
After Service Start OTP Verified Non-refundable (Service in progress) Full booking amount applies

2. 48-Hour Refund SLA

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment method (UPI, NetBanking, Debit/Credit Card) via Razorpay within 48 business hours. Depending on the customer's issuing bank, funds typically credit within 3–5 business days.

3. Statutory Non-Refundable Components

Where work has commenced or a technician has traveled to the premises, visiting inspection charges (₹49–₹99) and statutory GST (18%) on services already rendered are non-refundable in compliance with Indian taxation laws.
