Prominent In-App & Store Disclosure: This document explains exactly how and why BookUrTechnician (Customer App) and BookUrTechnician Partner (Technician App) access, collect, and use device location data, including foreground and background access.

1. Customer App Location Access (Foreground Only)

The BookUrTechnician Customer App accesses location data exclusively while the application is in active foreground use:

  • Service Address Resolution: Automatically detecting customer coordinates to populate the doorstep service address and verify operational coverage within serviceable city zones.
  • Technician Proximity Calculation: Calculating the distance to the nearest available domain technician (3 km, 5 km, 10 km, or 15 km) and providing live estimated arrival times.
  • Zero Background Tracking: The Customer App never requests ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION and stops accessing GPS coordinates immediately when the app is minimized or closed.

2. Technician App Location Access (Foreground & Background on Duty)

The BookUrTechnician Partner App requires location access (including while the app is in the background or screen locked) only while the partner has explicitly toggled their status to "ON DUTY":

  • Automatic Nearby Job Matching: Discovering nearby service requests within the technician's 15 km operational perimeter and delivering real-time siren dispatch alerts.
  • Customer Navigation & Live Tracking: Transmitting encrypted GPS coordinates to the booked customer during active transit so the customer can track the technician's arrival progress.
  • Doorstep Arrival Verification: Automated 500-meter arrival geofencing to verify the partner has reached the customer doorstep before enabling Start OTP entry.
  • Immediate Cessation When Off Duty: When the technician toggles their duty status to "OFFLINE", all foreground service and background location polling ceases immediately.

3. Privacy & Data Protection Safeguards

  • Location data is never sold, leased, or shared with advertising networks or third-party marketing brokers.
  • During dispatch proposals, only approximate locality names (e.g. "Salt Lake Sector 5, Kolkata") are presented; exact street addresses are revealed only after a technician accepts the job offer.
  • Location history updates are retained strictly for active transit safety and dispute resolution, and are permanently erased or anonymized upon account deletion.
