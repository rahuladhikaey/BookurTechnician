1. Platform Marketplace Role

BookurTechnician operates as a digital technology marketplace connecting verified independent skilled technicians ("Service Partners") with customers seeking home repair, electrical, plumbing, appliance servicing, and maintenance solutions.

2. Booking and Dispatch State Machine

All service requests are governed by our real-time dispatch state machine:

  • Booking Creation: Authoritative backend calculation of base price, visiting inspection charges (₹49–₹99), and statutory GST (18%).
  • Dual OTP Doorstep Verification: Technicians cannot commence service without the customer providing the secure 4-digit Start OTP generated by the backend. Service cannot be marked completed without customer disclosure of the End OTP.
  • Mandatory Photo Proof: Technicians must submit photographic evidence of completed work with embedded GPS telemetry to validate service warranties.

3. 30-Day Service Guarantee

Qualified home repair bookings include a 30-day service revisit warranty against defects in workmanship. Warranty claims require verified start and end OTP records and validated after-service photo proof.

4. Partner KYC and Independent Contractor Status

Technicians operate as independent contractors and must submit government identity documents (Aadhaar, Voter Card, Driving License) and live selfies prior to verification. BookurTechnician conducts KYC checks but partners retain independent operational discretion.

5. Payments and Billing

Payments are collected digitally via certified payment gateways or in cash upon completion. Official digital GST tax invoices (SAC code 9987) are generated immediately upon verified service completion.
