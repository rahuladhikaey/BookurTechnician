1. Information We Collect

BookurTechnician collects and processes only the information necessary to provide on-demand home service discovery, automatic technician dispatch, real-time tracking, secure OTP authentication, and digital payment fulfillment:

  • Account & Profile Details: Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, customer service address, and technician declared skills.
  • Technician KYC Data: Government identity documents (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving License), live facial verification selfie, and bank UPI details exclusively for verified service partner onboarding and statutory compliance.
  • Precise & Coarse Location Data: Customer service location coordinates for technician dispatch matching. Technician real-time GPS location while on duty to calculate arrival ETAs and provide live transit tracking to the assigned customer.
  • Job Proof & Media: Digital before-and-after work service photographs uploaded by technicians to record completed repairs and substantiate 30-day warranty claims.
  • Device & Notification Telemetry: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens, device OS version, and network status to deliver dispatch alerts and service progress notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Automatic Dispatch & Matching: Geospatial PostGIS calculation within a 3–15 km service radius to dispatch the nearest certified domain-specialist technician.
  • Safety & Dual OTP Verification: Secure 4-digit start OTP and completion OTP generation to verify the technician's arrival and authorize job completion at the customer doorstep.
  • Payment Processing: Seamless online payments and instant partner payouts facilitated through certified RBI-compliant payment gateways (Razorpay). We never store customer credit/debit card numbers or CVVs on our servers.
  • Real-Time Transit Tracking: Displaying technician transit progress exclusively in the private Socket.IO room of the booked customer during active transit. Location updates cease immediately upon job completion or cancellation.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver real-time infrastructure, we collaborate with industry-standard, security-certified infrastructure partners under strict data-protection agreements:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud Firestore data synchronization, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Storage for KYC documents and service proofs, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for high-priority dispatch alarms.
  • Razorpay Software Private Limited: Payment collection, webhook signature verification, and automated refunds.
  • Google Maps Platform: Reverse geocoding and live navigational ETA calculation.

4. Data Security and Privacy Protection

We maintain strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized data access, leakage, or tampering:

  • All network communications are strictly enforced over HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and JWT authentication prevent any customer or technician from viewing another party's private data (zero IDOR vulnerabilities).
  • Technician location telemetry is scoped exclusively to authorized active booking rooms and automatically stripped of customer PII during sequential dispatch proposals.

5. Account Deletion and Data Retention Rights

Users have the unconditional right to delete their accounts and request data erasure at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Accessible directly within Customer App (Settings → Privacy Center → Delete Account) and Technician App (Profile → Privacy Policy & Delete Partner Account).
  • Public Web Portal: Accessible without installing the app at https://bookmytechnician-53d94.web.app/delete-account.html.
  • Statutory Retention: Legally mandated tax invoices, GST ledger records (SAC 9987), and completed payment transactions are retained for statutory audit compliance under applicable Indian tax and financial laws with personal identifying details masked.
