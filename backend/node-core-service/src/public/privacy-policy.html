Entity: BookurTechnician Technologies Private Limited ("BookurTechnician", "we", "our", or "us")

Notice under Section 5 of DPDP Act 2023: This Privacy Policy informs you about the categories of personal data collected, the specific lawful purpose of processing, and how you can exercise your statutory rights of access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023).

1. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the personal information strictly necessary for the fulfillment of on-demand home technical services:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, profile photo (optional).
  • Location Data (Approximate & Precise GPS): Real-time location coordinates to connect you with the nearest online verified technician and provide live on-map tracking.
  • Service Details: Selected appliance type, fault descriptions, scheduled service time, and diagnostic notes.
  • Payment Information: Transactions are processed through RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (Razorpay). Full card credentials and CVVs are NEVER captured or stored on our servers.
  • Technician Partner KYC (Partner App Only): Government ID verification (Aadhaar / Voter ID / PAN), skill certification, and bank account / UPI VPA for payout processing. All sensitive KYC and UPI details are stored in an encrypted vault.

2. Lawful Purposes of Processing

  • Assigning and dispatching the nearest verified technician to your doorstep.
  • Displaying live distance, estimated arrival time (ETA), and interactive map tracking.
  • Enabling privacy-safe, masked telephone calling between customer and technician.
  • Generating statutory GST-compliant invoices and providing a 30-day service warranty.
  • Preventing fraud, identity theft, and duplicate bookings.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose information only to:

  • The Assigned Technician: Name, service address, and issue description solely for the fulfillment of the accepted booking.
  • Payment Aggregators (Razorpay): Transaction tokens for checkout processing.
  • Cloud & Map Infrastructure (Google Cloud / Firebase / Google Maps): Secure encrypted hosting, database persistence, and geocoding.
  • Legal / Statutory Authorities: When strictly mandated by Indian law or court order.

4. Security Safeguards (Section 8(5))

We enforce rigorous safeguards to prevent unauthorized data loss or breach:

  • End-to-end encryption in transit via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Data at rest encrypted using industry-standard AES-256.
  • Cryptographic SHA-256 hashing for all OTP verification codes.
  • Role-based access controls and least-privilege Firestore security rules.

5. Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act 2023)

Under Sections 11, 12, 13, and 14 of the DPDP Act 2023, you hold the following rights:

  • Right to Access: View a summary of your personal data and identities of processors.
  • Right to Correction: Update inaccurate phone numbers, addresses, or profile details in-app.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): Request complete account and data wipe via https://bookurtechnician.online/delete-account or inside the app.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke optional permissions anytime in device settings.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

6. Protection of Children (Section 9)

Our services are strictly meant for individuals aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly process personal data of children, nor do we perform behavioral tracking or targeted advertising directed at minors.
